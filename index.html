ABOUT

VIZ LIZER is a specialized freelance 3D art and visual effects studio focusing on procedural simulations, real-time environments, and comprehensive post-production pipelines. Operating at the intersection of technical execution and creative storytelling, every asset and scene is meticulously engineered for real-time engines, cinematic animations, and digital production.

Houdini Blender Unreal Engine Nuke DaVinci Resolve Substance Painter

TECHNICAL WORKFLOW

Leveraging advanced procedural nodes in Houdini and geometry node networks in Blender, the simulation workflow focuses on dynamic particle systems, fluid interactions, and physics-driven particle simulations. Detailed surface texturing, gradient maps, and material shading are meticulously painted using Substance Painter. For final delivery, sequences are brought into DaVinci Resolve for ACEScg color grading and high-end video editing, ensuring seamless integration across real-time engines like Unreal Engine and deep compositing workflows in Nuke.
